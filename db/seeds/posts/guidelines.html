These Posting Guidelines govern what kind of questions may be asked on this Kaspa Q&A site. By posting here, you agree to follow these rules so that the site remains a high-quality, technical resource for the whole community.

1. No Price Speculation

This site is for technical questions and answers, not speculation. Do not post about price predictions, trading strategies, or market discussions. Such posts will be removed.

2. Focus on Technology and Theory

Questions should relate to Kaspa’s protocol, consensus, implementation, and usage. Do not ask about project roadmaps, release dates, or timelines. Keep your questions technical, theoretical, or about practical troubleshooting.

3. No Lost Wallet Recovery

We cannot help you recover lost wallets, private keys, or seed phrases. For your own security, never share private keys or seeds here. Posts requesting help with lost funds will be removed.

4. No Exchange Recommendations

Do not ask which exchange is best for buying or selling Kaspa. Such questions quickly become outdated and are outside the scope of this site.

5. Questions Must Be in English

All questions and answers should be written in English. Supporting multiple languages would be difficult to moderate fairly and consistently. If there is enough demand and available staff in the future, we may add dedicated communities for other languages.

6. Questions Must Be Specific

Questions should be clear and specific, not vague or overly broad. A good question can be answered concretely and will be useful to future readers. Avoid general requests like "Explain Kaspa" or "Tell me everything about wallets" — instead, focus on a precise aspect or problem.

Summary

This site is for clear, technical questions about Kaspa. No price speculation, no roadmap speculation, no exchange recommendations, no lost wallet recovery requests, and all posts must be in English. Questions should be specific and concrete, so answers can be useful for everyone. By following these guidelines, you help maintain Kaspa Q&A as a valuable resource for the community.
