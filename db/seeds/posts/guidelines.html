These Posting Guidelines govern what kind of questions may be asked on this Kaspa Q&A site. By posting here, you agree to follow these rules so that the site remains a high-quality, technical resource for the whole community.

1. No Price Speculation

This site is for technical questions and answers, not speculation. Do not post about price predictions, trading strategies, or market discussions. Such posts will be removed.

2. Focus on Technology and Theory

Questions should relate to Kaspa’s protocol, consensus, implementation, and usage. Do not ask about project roadmaps, release dates, or timelines. Keep your questions technical, theoretical, or about practical troubleshooting.

3. No Lost Wallet Recovery

We cannot help you recover lost wallets, private keys, or seed phrases. For your own security, never share private keys or seeds here. Posts requesting help with lost funds will be removed.

Summary

This site is for clear, technical questions about Kaspa. No price speculation, no roadmap speculation, and no lost wallet recovery requests. By following these guidelines, you help maintain Kaspa Q&A as a valuable resource for everyone.
